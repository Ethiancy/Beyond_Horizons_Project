In making this website, I hope to teach others about a passing hobby of mine; black holes. These wonderous celestial bodies have captivated me since I first heard about them in high school, and have kept my interest ever since. As such, I hope to pass on my interest to you, dear reader.

Delving Deeper into Black Holes

Black holes are the cosmic equivalent of a Golden Goose to scientists. They are both difficult to find, and can answer many secrets related to the early creation of our universe. To give a short introduction for the sizes involved, the sun is 1 M☉ or one solar mass. This is how all stars and black holes are compared, in relation to the sun. How scientists come to measure and learn of the mass of many different celestial bodies varies, and coming to understand the size of black holes especially is difficult because most don't directly generate light, although those that such as Sagittarius A produce some of the brightest celestial beacons in our universe.

Despite how difficult they often are to find, they may be the key to understanding our universe and the particles within. This doesn't just stop at the inception of our universe, black holes may be or explain what dark matter is, they may be the greatest garbage cans in the universe, and they may even used center of one of the most terrifying and awesomely powerful bombs ever thought up.
